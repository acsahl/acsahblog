Everything about the blog that is not a post lives in `src/site.config.js`. Open it and you will find:

- **name** is the text in the browser tab.
- **logo** is the wordmark in the top-left corner.
- **tagline** is the line under your name on the About page.
- **author** holds your name, your photo and the short bio on the homepage.
- **about** is the text of the About page, one entry for each paragraph.
- **links** are the links in the footer and on the About page.

## Add your photo

Until you add one, the round badge shows the first letter of your name. To use a photo, put it in `src/assets`, then import it at the top of the config file and set it as `photo`:

```js
import photo from './assets/me.jpg'

export const site = {
  author: {
    name: 'Acsah',
    photo: photo,
  },
}
```

## Change the fonts

The three fonts are set at the top of `src/styles/tokens.css`. The headline font is called `--serif`, the main font is `--sans` and the small label font is `--mono`.
