This is a starter post, written so the blog has something to show while it is new. Delete it once you have a post of your own.

## What works today

- The homepage shows one featured post, then the six newest posts.
- The category buttons filter the posts without reloading the page.
- **All posts** lists everything and has a search box.
- Every post gets its own page, with links to the post before and after it.

## What to change first

1. Open `src/site.config.js` and set your name, tagline and bio.
2. Write a post. The next starter post shows how.
3. Delete the starter posts in `src/posts` when you no longer need them.

> Everything you are likely to edit lives in two places: the config file and the posts folder.

The rest of the starter posts cover one job each: writing a post, adding pictures, categories, the newsletter form and putting the site online.
